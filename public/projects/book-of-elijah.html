005 / BOE

Interrogate the lens.

Book of Elijah treats translation as a visible process rather than a finished authority. The KJV remains intact for comparison while a new rendering is built from source-language witnesses and tested against non-biblical ancient usage.

The application makes uncertainty legible. Parallel and single-edition views, persistent highlights, full-book navigation, search, and red-letter passages turn a translation experiment into a practical reading ecosystem.
