The idea

006 / CA

Less deciding.
More playing.

Cupid’s Arrow turns indecision into momentum. Randomized prompts and simple timers create a lightweight game loop that keeps two people present, playful, and engaged.

The beta is deliberately direct: choose the experience, draw the next prompt, agree together, and let the timer carry the moment forward.
