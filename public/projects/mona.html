006 / MONA

Go beyond the known.
Keep the evidence visible.

MONA opens the full periodic table as a computational research surface. Researchers can select any elemental combination, inspect the available cancer-response evidence, and see clearly where a result is measured, inferred, or projected.

The system is designed for disciplined exploration: flexible enough to form new hypotheses, rigorous enough to expose coverage and confidence, and careful never to disguise a computational lead as a clinical conclusion.

NOW

Every element in reach

METHOD

Evidence has a shape

Direct, hybrid, and projected results keep provenance and confidence visible.

PRINCIPLE

Hypothesis, not diagnosis

MONA is a research instrument built to guide investigation, not replace clinical judgment.
